Why this model exists

Cloud agents are powerful — and expensive, laggy across the ocean, and a non-starter when the data cannot leave the machine. Always-on local agents need a different design point: enough intelligence for multi-step tool work, small enough for a Mac or single consumer GPU, trained to recover when tools fail instead of freezing mid-task.

On-device by design

Quantized under ~20 GB with headroom for KV, vision, and a speculative drafter on 24–32 GB boxes.

Agent loops, not chat toys

Function calling, long-horizon plans, and deliberate failure recovery as a training target.

Multimodal perception

2B Perception Encoder for interleaved images (and video frames) plus text — see, then act.

Speed knobs

Controllable reasoning strength + optional DFlash block drafter for structured / coding decode.

Tune the sliders to see whether a local always-on agent fits.

  • Full precision 30B ≈ 55+ GB — out of reach for most laptops.
  • ~4-bit brings the LM under ~20 GB with minimal agentic degradation (per Meta).
  • Leave room for KV cache + tools + OS on a 24–32 GB envelope.
